## 1. Executive Summary of Strategic Pivot

In strict accordance with user directives, all premature application code and scaffolding have been cleared from disk to focus entirely on the **deep architectural study, behavioral economics, structural UI/UX, civic gamification, and enterprise ERP workflows**:
1. **Purged Legalistic Jargon:** Completely removed all references to Algerian Law 23-07. Focus shifted to academic mastery, intrinsic scientific motivation, and real-world project portfolios.
2. **Ergonomic Center-Anchor Navigation:** Redesigned navigation around **Home (الرئيسية) as the exact middle anchor (Tab 3 out of 5)**:
   - `[1: الأنشطة (Activities) | 2: آفاق (Explore) | 3: الرئيسية (Home - Center) | 4: المهام والعمليات (Missions) | 5: هويتي (Profile)]`.
3. **Institutional Mindset Paradigm:** Replaced the patronizing language of casual volunteering ("تطوع") with **«المهام والعمليات» (Missions & Operations)**.
4. **Student KYC Architecture:** Designed the 5-day $\times$ 9-slot academic availability heatmap (Sunday–Thursday, 08:00–17:00), preventing mission dispatching during lectures and automatically pausing tasks during exam periods.
5. **Consumer Gamification Benchmarks (Duolingo, Strava, GitHub):**
   - Daily loss-aversion streak loops (`🔥`) with Archimedes Shields (streak freezes).
   - Circadian morning and evening chests with academic quiet hours (23:00–07:30).
   - 30-member Dunbar cohort league ladders (Subatomic to Quantum League).
   - Multi-factor **Member of the Month (عضو الشهر)** formula ($S_{\text{MoM}}$) with a 50% recency dampener to prevent senior monopoly, plus a dedicated "Rising Star" category for junior members.
   - GitHub-inspired 52-week scientific contribution heatmap.
6. **Member-Initiated QR Attendance Check-In:**
   - 15-second dynamic rolling TOTP QR code projected on amphi screens.
   - Attendees scan the screen from their seats using the in-app camera, eliminating 30-minute door queues.
7. **Dedicated Admin Management Platform (QAMP):**
   - Web/tablet/mobile console for Executive Board (President, VP, SG) and Cell Leads (Logistics, Media, Tech, HR, Scientific Committees).
   - Modules: Workshops & Projector Mode, Missions Dispatcher, Member KYC Intelligence Directory, Gamification Engine, Badges Desk, and Annual Moral Report compiler.
